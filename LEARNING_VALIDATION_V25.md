# v25 — Guided first lesson and honest learning evidence

## Release scope
- Home recommends a five-minute first lesson and changes to Continue after starting.
- New Daily 5 material is introduced before a question. Existing review material remains a retrieval opportunity.
- Review targets are saved for the session. Show me reveals the actual review answer, not the new lesson's different focus word.
- The final phrase task reuses the lesson’s focus word and taught example rather than switching to an unrelated scenario. Full conversation scenarios remain available separately.
- Incorrect answers explain the relevant translation or pattern.
- Completed adventure worlds unlock a second authored five-question bank (15 new questions total); active sessions retain their bank after reload.
- Stars remain completion rewards. Independent choices exclude explicit hints and retries and are displayed separately. Existing stars are preserved; historical independent scores are not invented.
- Daily lessons can check previous lesson vocabulary after 24 hours, and again after three days. Recognition with choices is evidence of recognition, not proof of free recall or fluency. Help and retries are recorded separately.
- All evidence remains on-device and is included by the existing ldna-prefixed backup mechanism.

## Beginner study — not yet conducted
Recruit 5–10 consenting beginners; supervise children appropriately. Do not collect names, recordings or unnecessary personal information.
1. Without coaching, ask them to start learning from Home. Record whether they find the lesson and where they hesitate.
2. Ask them to explain the new word/pattern in their own words.
3. Observe one complete lesson and an adventure. Record misunderstood instructions and help used.
4. Ask what five stars mean. Check they understand completion differs from independent learning.
5. After 24–72 hours, ask them to recall the taught word without choices. Then show a new example of the pattern and ask them to apply it. Finally allow the app's supported recognition check.
6. Record aggregate completion, independent recall and transfer counts separately, including participant count and interval. Report uncertainty; do not claim proven effectiveness from a small pilot.

## Release limitations
Human Spanish-educator review, real-device speech checks and actual learner validation remain pending. The replay bank is finite. Later recognition checks do not certify CEFR levels or accent quality.
