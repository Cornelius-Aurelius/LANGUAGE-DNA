# Everyday 100 + 40-Question Game Mode

Generated: 2026-10-07

## Everyday 100

The Everyday 100 is a deliberately small, English-first beginner layer. It contains 100 words and short phrases chosen for day-to-day usefulness: greetings, politeness, people, questions, communication repair, needs, actions, time, places, food/drink, safety and money.

The list is **not presented as an exact British-English corpus-frequency ranking**. Its order is a LanguageDNA teaching priority designed around immediate usefulness and low cognitive load.

Each item contains:

- English first;
- Spanish equivalent;
- a short real-use example;
- learner-level label;
- usefulness score/band;
- word-family information;
- morphology/forms where useful;
- usage notes and ambiguity warnings where useful;
- Spanish speech playback;
- a local “familiar” marker.

The learning browser displays 20 items at a time and supports search and category filters so the learner is not confronted with all 100 at once.

## Meaning traps / false friends

The Everyday learning area also includes a compact expandable set of common English-Spanish meaning traps, including actual, embarazada, asistir, librería, carpeta and sensible. These are taught separately from the core 100 so the beginner list stays simple.

## 40-question Game Mode

Every difficulty level contains exactly 40 multiple-choice questions with four answer choices.

During the test:

- selected answers are shown only as selected;
- no right/wrong marking is revealed;
- the learner can move backward or jump to any numbered question;
- all 40 questions must be answered before scoring.

At the end:

- the score is calculated out of 40;
- **37/40 (92.5%) is required to pass**;
- every question is displayed in a full review;
- green indicates correct and red indicates incorrect;
- the learner sees their answer, the correct answer when missed, and a short explanation;
- the review can be filtered to only missed concepts;
- failed levels can be retried;
- passed levels unlock the next difficulty.

## Difficulty ladder

1. **Everyday Essentials** — direct English → Spanish recognition.
2. **Useful Phrases & Actions** — questions, needs and common actions.
3. **Real-life Sentences** — English sentence → Spanish sentence recognition.
4. **Spanish → English** — reverse recognition without seeing English first.
5. **Mixed Real-life Challenge** — mixed words, phrases, sentences and reverse recognition.

Best scores, unlocked levels and recent attempts are stored locally in browser storage.
