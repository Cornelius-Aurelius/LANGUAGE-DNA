# Xabi — official learning mascot

**Approved mascot:** Xabi (pronounced approximately “SHAH-bee”), the blue, orange and white hybrid learner with parrot feathers, a dolphin-like beak, friendly ape-like expressions, glowing bee wings, playful octopus tentacles, glasses, books, a hoodie, varsity jacket, cargo shorts and sneakers.

Use Xabi as the same recognisable character throughout the BluXabi learner experience: the home welcome, daily progress, friendly feedback, adventure success, and learning celebrations. Keep him curious, kind, funny and encouraging to learners of all ages. His outfit should **not** show words, logos or labels.

## Canonical assets

- `xabi-hero.webp` — Xabi's approved full-body pose on a genuinely transparent background, optimised for browser use.
- `xabi-face.webp` — cropped portrait of the same approved Xabi image for compact UI.

Both assets are derived directly from the user-approved character image (fin-free version). Do not substitute other mascot variants unless requested. The site is branded **BluXabi**, while the character's name is **Xabi**. Their names are related, but they serve different roles.

Web accessibility: meaningful hero art gets a descriptive alt text, and repeated decorative faces use empty alt text. No mascot visual should obscure primary learning buttons or answer text.

## BluXabi logo (reframed October 2026)

- `xabi-logo.webp` — brand-header portrait with safe space for Xabi's full blue-and-orange crest and a softly rounded mint background.
- `xabi-icon-192-v2.png` and `xabi-icon-512-v2.png` — matching favicon/PWA icon portraits at native resolutions, without an artificial bottom stripe.
- The mark uses a light, even shadow rather than the previous thick green bottom edge.
- These portraits are crops of Xabi's existing **approved original image**, not a change to his appearance. The original `xabi-hero.webp` and `xabi-face.webp` are retained for lessons.
